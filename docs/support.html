Support

How to use it

  1. Open a streaming site, start playback, then click the Playbright icon.
  2. Move Dark overlay reduction from Original toward Remove.
  3. Use the switch at the top of the popup to turn it on or off for the current site, or press Alt+Shift+Y.
  4. Type a name and click Save to keep the current level as a preset. You can keep up to 8.
  5. Click Pick UI to hide, then click a control or panel on the page. Press Esc to cancel.
  6. Click Reset hidden elements for this site to bring hidden UI back.

Troubleshooting

  • Nothing happens. Start video playback first, then click Rescan overlays.
  • "Unavailable on this page". Chrome blocks extensions on internal pages such as chrome://.
  • A site stopped working. Sites change their player markup. Use Pick UI to hide for anything Playbright cannot identify.
  • After updating from source. Reload the extension at chrome://extensions and refresh the tab.

Report an issue

Open an issue at github.com/HumayounBaig/PlayBright/issues, or email Humayoun.mani@gmail.com. Please include the site and what you expected.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. An optional tip link may appear in the popup, but nothing is locked.

Does it collect data?

No. Settings stay in your browser. See the privacy policy.

Why does it need access to all sites?

Overlays can appear on any streaming site. It stays idle on pages with no video.

Does it touch the video itself?

No. It never reads or changes video pixels. It fades separate overlay elements and full-size gradient layers, and does not fade a container that holds the video.

Why doesn't it hide player buttons automatically?

It avoids hiding interactive controls on purpose. Use the picker for the ones you want gone.
